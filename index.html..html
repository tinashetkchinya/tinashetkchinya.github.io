Bio

A Psychology and Political Science graduate of the University of Botswana, Tinashe works where individual behaviour meets the systems around it.

Psychology gave her the tools to understand motivation, habit and change. Political Science showed her how policy, power and institutions frame what people are able to choose. She is now building a career in research and policy advocacy, pairing her degree with data analytics, Excel and Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL), and exploring how AI and data can improve public policy.

Data and decisionsThe poultry project

What a live bird really costs

Started while completing her studies, Tinashe's poultry project tests a simple idea: tracking the numbers changes the decisions a small business makes. Using Excel, she recorded sales, inventory, feed and expenses, and learned where money and birds were being lost. Her takeaway: many small producers stay busy but unrewarded because they do not track data or separate personal and business finances.
